Subtitles for the window seat.

SkyAtlas tells you which side to sit on, what will pass beneath the wing and at what minute, and the story behind each place. In the air it works in airplane mode, with no account.

BEFORE YOU FLY
• Add a flight by airports, date and departure time, or scan your boarding pass, or import its Apple Wallet file.
• Your phone builds the route itself, around any airspace closed to your carrier.
• Window advice: which side to ask for, and how much of the flight is in daylight. A day before, at check-in time, a reminder names that side and what it will show, unless your seat is already known or there is little to see.
• Stories written for the view from the window: why each place is worth a look, exactly what to look for, and a few facts you’ll remember. For the most notable places they come built in, in all six languages.
• On Wi-Fi, it also saves photo galleries from Wikimedia Commons, Wikipedia articles for everything else, and an offline map of the route.

ALONG THE WAY
From some 9,300 peaks, volcanoes, ranges, deserts, seas, lakes, islands, rivers, glaciers and cities: which side, at what minute, how far away, and how long each stays in view. Plus borders, the Equator, the tropics, polar circles, the Date Line, sunrise and sunset on board, the start of the descent.
And the history beneath you: the Silk Road, ancient capitals, old trade and pilgrim routes, told as you pass them.

IN THE AIR
• About 15 minutes after departure, “Airborne?” appears on your lock screen: tap “Took off” and the flight starts. From then on, everything runs in airplane mode.
• Out of the window now: left, right and below, and what’s next, with a countdown.
• Alerts for the three best moments on your side (or up to six); tap one to open that place’s story. And silence when there’s nothing to see.
• GPS refines the position whenever your phone has a fix.
• Guess what’s coming, or let the audio guide read each place aloud.
• See it with your own eyes? Tap “I see it” and the place turns gold.

AFTER LANDING
• Every country you flew over is stamped in your atlas; every place you saw turns gold.
• Experience, levels and ranks, from Newcomer to Legend of the Skies, and 30 achievements earned simply by flying.
• A short quiz about the flight and the places you passed, each answer explained, and a postcard to send with “we landed”.
