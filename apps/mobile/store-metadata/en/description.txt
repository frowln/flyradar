SkyAtlas — Discover the world from above

Turn every flight into a journey of discovery. SkyAtlas tracks your position offline and reveals what's below — mountains, cities, lakes, historic landmarks — with photos, facts, and stories from Wikipedia.

✈ Offline flight tracking — no internet needed in the air
🗺 Real-time discovery — POI cards appear as you fly over them
🏆 Collect the world — 46 achievements, levels, ranks
📊 Year Wrapped — your annual flying recap
🎙 Listen mode — text-to-speech narration
🌍 6 languages — English, Russian, German, French, Spanish, Japanese

Pre-flight: download your flight package with route, POIs, weather forecast for destination, and packing suggestions.

In-flight: real Apple Maps view, your plane on the route, POI cards every few minutes with photos and fascinating facts.

Post-flight: see what you discovered, share your achievements, watch your collection grow.

Free to use. SkyAtlas Pro unlocks unlimited POIs, all achievements, and history.
