# Privacy Policy

**SkyAtlas**
Last updated: May 2026

## 1. Introduction

SkyAtlas ("we", "our", or "us") operates the SkyAtlas mobile application (the "App"). This Privacy Policy explains what information we collect, how we use it, and your rights regarding your data.

By using the App, you agree to the collection and use of information in accordance with this policy.

## 2. Data We Collect

### 2.1 Account Information
SkyAtlas does not require account creation. You use the App anonymously. No name, email, or personal identifier is required to use the App.

If you choose to contact us for support, we collect only the email address you provide in that communication.

### 2.2 Flight Data
- **Local storage only**: Your flight history, collected POIs, and achievements are stored locally on your device using SQLite.
- **Backend cache**: When pre-fetching flight packages (routes, POIs, weather), anonymized requests are sent to our backend servers. These requests contain a flight number and departure date but no user identifier.
- We do not store flight history on our servers beyond the cache TTL (24 hours).

### 2.3 Location Data
- The App uses your device's GPS **only when you explicitly start a flight session**.
- Location is used solely to display nearby points of interest during the flight.
- Location data is **never sent to our servers** and is not stored beyond the active session.
- We request location access only at the moment you confirm takeoff.

### 2.4 Purchase Information
In-app purchases (SkyAtlas Pro) are processed through Apple's App Store. We do not receive or store your payment card information. Purchase status is managed via RevenueCat (see Third-Party Services).

### 2.5 Crash and Analytics Data
We collect anonymized crash reports and usage analytics to improve the App. This data does not include personally identifiable information.

## 5. Your Rights

Regardless of your location, you have the right to:

- **Access**: Request a copy of any data we hold about you.
- **Deletion**: Request deletion of any data associated with you.
- **Portability**: Export your local flight data via the in-app export feature (Settings → Export Data).
- **Opt-out of analytics**: Disable analytics in Settings → Privacy.

Since most data is stored locally on your device, you can delete it at any time by removing the App or clearing its data.
