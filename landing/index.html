Features

Everything you need
at 35,000 feet

✈

Offline flight tracking

No internet required in the air. Download your flight package before boarding — route, POIs, weather — and fly completely offline.

🗺

Real-time discovery

POI cards appear automatically as you fly over mountains, cities, lakes, and historic sites. Photos and Wikipedia facts delivered mid-flight.

🏆

Collect the world

46 achievements to unlock, country stamps to collect, and a growing rank system. Every flight adds to your passport.

📊

Year Wrapped

Your annual flying recap — countries visited, distance traveled, POIs discovered, and highlights from every journey.

🎙

Listen mode

Sit back and let SkyAtlas narrate your journey. Text-to-speech reads POI descriptions aloud so you never miss a discovery.

🌍

6 languages

Full support for English, Russian, German, French, Spanish, and Japanese. The world is yours, in your language.
