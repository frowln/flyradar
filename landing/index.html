DETAILS — FOR THE THOROUGH

Common questions, honest answers.

Does it really work offline? +
Yes. Before you board, SkyAtlas downloads your route, every POI along it, every photograph, every fact, and the position engine itself. In flight, your phone simulates your position using the route and time of takeoff — no GPS, no Wi-Fi, no signal required. Live mode is optionally available if your flight has Wi-Fi, but it's never required.
How does SkyAtlas know what's below me? +
We sample your great-circle route at intervals, query a curated geographic database (GeoNames, with editorial cleanup), and pull the relevant Wikipedia entry in your language. Then our content engine picks three things worth knowing, one photograph that does the place justice, and presents it in a card that fades up at exactly the moment your aircraft passes overhead.
Is my data private? +
Your flight history, achievements, and collection live on your phone and in your private iCloud. We don't sell behavioural data and we don't run third-party advertising trackers. Crash reporting and basic analytics (Sentry, PostHog) are anonymous and configurable in Settings. Full details in our Privacy Policy.
What languages are supported? +
The full app is translated into English, Russian, German, French, Spanish, and Japanese. POI descriptions are pulled in your language from Wikipedia when available, with a graceful English fallback for less-documented places.
Is there an Android version? +
Not yet. SkyAtlas is iOS-only at launch. An Android version is on the roadmap once the iOS app proves its value to enough travellers.
What about flight delays and gate changes? +
SkyAtlas isn't a flight-status app — that's Flighty's territory. We focus on the experience during the flight: what's below, what to look at, what to remember. Pre-flight delay alerts are on the roadmap for v1.2.
Can I import my flight history? +
Yes. SkyAtlas reads Apple Wallet boarding passes directly — share a `.pkpass` to the app and it parses the flight automatically. Bulk CSV import from TripIt and Flighty is planned for v1.1.
Who's behind SkyAtlas? +
A small independent team that flies too much, gets bored looking out the window, and wanted something better than the Sky Mall magazine. Built with care from scratch — no white-label, no platform play. Contact us.

LEGAL · LAST UPDATED 2026-05-20

Privacy Policy

SkyAtlas is built around a simple privacy principle: your travel history is yours. We don't sell behavioural data and we don't run third-party advertising trackers.

What we store on your device

Your flight history, achievements, settings, and offline content packages are stored locally using iOS SQLite and MMKV. They sync to your private iCloud when iCloud Backup is enabled.

What we send to our servers

When you add a flight, we send the flight number and date to our backend to fetch route geometry, POI metadata, and editorial content. No personal identifying information is transmitted with these requests.

Third-party services

  • Sentry — anonymous crash reports (opt-out in Settings)
  • PostHog — anonymous product analytics (opt-out in Settings)
  • RevenueCat — subscription management (App Store / Google Play receipts)
  • GeoNames, Wikipedia, OpenStreetMap, Open-Meteo — public data sources used to assemble flight packages

Your rights

You can disable analytics and crash reporting in Settings. You can delete all local data by uninstalling the app. For data deletion from our servers (limited to device tokens and cached flight lookups), email support@skyatlas.app.
